# 🧩 What Problems Does EdensFrequency Actually Solve?

We get asked this a lot, so here's the honest version instead of a mission-statement wave of the hand.

## 🎛️ Hardware Costs Money You Might Not Have

A real hardware sampler, a set of turntables and a mixer, a rack of samplers, that's real money before you've made a single beat. Even second-hand gear gets marked up once it has to be imported into Southern Africa, and shipping times and import duties make "just order it" a lot less simple than it sounds from the US or Europe. Boom Bap Producer Pads gives you the actual workflows, sampling, sequencing, scratching, playing samples across a keyboard, on a computer you probably already own.

## 🧩 One Tool, No Giant DAW Required

A full DAW is a lot to sit down with when what you actually want is to chop a break, learn to scratch, or play a vocal sample across a keyboard. Pads puts exactly those workflows in one place, so you're not hunting through menus meant for orchestral scoring or mixing engineers just to get a kick and snare pattern going. It used to be three separate products; see **[what moved where](ONE-PRODUCT.md)**.

## 💸 Cost Isn't the Gate

Pads is free to use, personally or commercially, and we don't charge our users. Other people now make similar products to sell; that doesn't change our objective or our values. See the **[license FAQ](faqs/IS-IT-FREE.md)** for what the license does and doesn't cover.

## 🛠️ Support Is an Actual Person

Report a bug here and you're talking to the person who wrote the code, not a ticket queue routed through three time zones. See **[reporting bugs](faqs/REPORTING-BUGS.md)** if something breaks, it gets looked at directly.

## 🌍 Proving the Build Layer Isn't Empty

We went looking for African-built DAWs and VST instruments before starting this and came up short. See **[About](ABOUT.md)** for what we actually found. Every product here is one more piece of evidence that the tools themselves, not just the content made with foreign tools, can be built from here too.
