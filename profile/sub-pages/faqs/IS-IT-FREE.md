## 💰 Is It Free? Can I Use It Commercially?

Yes. We don't charge our users, and that isn't changing: other people now make similar products to sell, but that doesn't change our objective or our values. You can install and use compiled builds of Boom Bap Producer Pads, VST3, CLAP or standalone, for your own music, personal or commercial. You don't need to credit us in tracks you make with it.

A few things the license doesn't cover:

- Redistributing, selling, or rehosting the plugin binaries yourself. Get them from the official Releases page on the product's repo.
- Reverse-engineering or decompiling a compiled build.
- The "Boom Bap Producer" name, its UI, or its bundled assets. Those stay with the creator (AdamNubian, distributed by EdensFrequency).
- Any warranty. Everything ships as is, and these products are still in active testing, see **[Development Status](../technical/DEVELOPMENT-STATUS.md)**.

The license terms may be updated as the project grows (the full terms are in the repo), but charging our users isn't part of the plan. If you need something outside normal personal or commercial use, reach out directly instead of assuming.

The full terms live in the [Pads repo](https://github.com/edensfrequency/boom-bap-producer-pads/blob/main/LICENSE.md). The discontinued [Decks](https://github.com/edensfrequency/boom-bap-producer-decks/blob/main/LICENSE.md) and [Key Sampler](https://github.com/edensfrequency/boom-bap-producer-key-sampler/blob/main/LICENSE.md) keep their own.
