## 🥇🥈🥉 What's the Difference Between Pads, Decks, and Key Sampler?

They're three separate instruments that solve different problems. Nothing stops you running all three at once.

**[Pads](../products/pads/boom-bap-producer-pads.md)** is for building a beat: a pad grid you trigger samples from, plus a step sequencer to arrange what you've chopped. If your question is "how do I make a drum pattern," start here.

**[Decks](../products/decks/boom-bap-producer-decks.md)** is for performing on a track you've already got: two turntable-style decks, a crossfader, and scratch control. If your question is "how do I scratch or mix between two tracks," this is it.

**[Key Sampler](../products/key-sampler/boom-bap-producer-key-sampler.md)** is for turning your own audio into an instrument: map samples across a keyboard's key zones and play them back melodically. If your question is "how do I play this sample like a synth," this is the one.

A rough way to think about it: Pads is rhythm, Decks is performance, Keys is melody. See **[our direction](../ABOUT.md)** for how they fit into the bigger picture.
