## 🥇 Pads, Decks, or Key Sampler: Which One Do I Want?

**Pads.** Decks and Key Sampler are discontinued, and everything they did now lives inside **[Boom Bap Producer Pads](../products/pads/boom-bap-producer-pads.md)**:

- Building a beat: the pads, the step sequencer, the 808 bass lane, and the arranger.
- Scratching and mixing between two tracks (what Decks did): the TURNTABLE tab, with two decks, a crossfader, scratch patterns, hot cues and stems.
- Playing a sample like an instrument (what Key Sampler did): any pad plays across the keyboard, and SYNTH turns any sound into a wavetable.

See **[what moved where](../ONE-PRODUCT.md)** for the full story.
