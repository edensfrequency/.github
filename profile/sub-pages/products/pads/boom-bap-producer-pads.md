## 🥇 Boom Bap Producer Pads

### Pad-grid sampling · sequencing · performance

Everything in one plugin for beatmakers who want a fast, hands-on workflow: a pad sampler and step sequencer, two turntables with stems and scratching, a synth with chords and an arp, an 808 bass lane, a mixer, a sample crate, and an arranger that turns loops into songs. Decks and Key Sampler now live in here (**[what moved where](../../ONE-PRODUCT.md)**).

Runs inside a VST3 or CLAP host, or on its own as a standalone app. Also has a one-click Windows installer - see [how do I install](../../faqs/HOW-DO-I-INSTALL.md) for details.

**Status:** 🔧 Active development - free, and growing

**Current version:** `v1.167.1`
