# 📣 Everything in One: Boom Bap Producer Pads

We've discontinued **Boom Bap Producer Decks** and **Boom Bap Producer Key Sampler**. Not because they didn't work, but because everything they did now lives in one place: **[Boom Bap Producer Pads](products/pads/boom-bap-producer-pads.md)**.

One install, one window, and the parts work together: chop the record on the deck straight onto the pads, grab what you just played and scratch it, then sequence it all into a song.

---

## 🔀 What Moved Where

| Was | Now, in Pads |
|---|---|
| **Decks**: two turntables, a crossfader, scratching | The **TURNTABLE** tab: two decks, a crossfader with a scratch curve, scratch patterns, performances like Transform and Flare in time with the beat, hot cues, Brake and Spinback, and DJ controller support |
| **Decks**: stems for vocal work | **Separate** on the deck: vocals, drums, bass and the rest, each on, off or solo |
| **Key Sampler**: playing a sample across the keyboard | Any pad plays across the keyboard, and **SYNTH** turns any sound into a wavetable |
| (new) | The step sequencer, an 808 **BASS** lane, chords and an arp on **SYNTH**, **KEYS**, the **MIXER**, **DISCOVER** and **ARRANGE** |

The Decks and Key Sampler repos stay up with their last releases, but they won't get updates. For anything new, use Pads.

---

## 💛 Still Free

We still don't charge our users. We've noticed other people now making similar products to sell, and that's their call, but it doesn't change our objective or our mission and values.

---

## 🌱 What's Next

We'll keep growing Pads, led by what you report and what we need when we make music with it ourselves.

The most likely next venture is producing preset and sample kits: sounds made for the way Pads works.
